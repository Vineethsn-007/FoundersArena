01 — The Event

Turn an idea into a product. A product into a brand. A brand into a business.

THE FOUNDER'S ARENA is a high-energy, two-day startup-building hackathon and Shark Tank-style pitching competition. Teams think like entrepreneurs, build like innovators, and pitch like founders.

Twenty teams. One real-world problem each. A working product, a startup brand, a chart presentation, a promo reel — and a panel of judges acting as investors.

03 — Your Team

Five founders. Five roles.

Every member must understand the whole product — judges may question anyone.

CEO

Chief Executive Officer

  • Introduces the startup and its vision
  • Explains the business concept
  • Presents mission and long-term goals

CTO

Chief Technology Officer

  • Explains architecture and tech stack
  • Demonstrates the product
  • Shows feasibility of the implementation

CPO

Chief Product Officer

  • Presents the problem and solution
  • Walks through features and user experience
  • Highlights the unique value

CFO

Chief Financial Officer

  • Explains business model and revenue
  • Presents assumptions and growth potential
  • States the investment request and its use

CMO

Chief Marketing Officer

  • Presents branding and marketing strategy
  • Defines target audience and acquisition
  • Produces the promotional reel

04 — The Two Days

Build on Day 1. Pitch on Day 2.

Day 1 — Build Day · November 2

Transform an idea into a functional product and a startup brand.

  1. Develop the product

    Implement core features and prepare a working demo.

  2. Create the brand

    Finalize a unique startup name, logo and visual identity.

  3. Prepare the chart

    A physical chart paper presentation covering name, domain, problem, solution, features, audience, USP, business model and visuals.

  4. Produce the reel

    A 30–60 second promotional reel that tells your story.

Submit: working product · startup name and branding · promotional reel · physical chart. At least two members stay at the workstation at all times.

Day 2 — The Investor Pitch · November 3

Convince the panel your startup deserves investment.

  1. Startup introduction

    Name, vision, team and roles.

  2. Problem and solution

    Who it affects, why it matters, how you solve it.

  3. Live product demo

    Show the working product and its key features.

  4. Chart presentation

    Use your physical chart to reinforce the story.

  5. Business and investment

    Revenue model, competitive edge, the ask, and future growth.

  6. Judges' Q&A

    Defend your product, tech, finances and market.

Event hours both days: 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM.
