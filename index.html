01 — The Event

Turn an idea into a product. A product into a brand. A brand into a business.

THE FOUNDER'S ARENA is a high-energy, two-day startup-building hackathon and Shark Tank-style pitching competition. Teams think like entrepreneurs, build like innovators, and pitch like founders.

Participation is based on a PPT selection round. Only the top 20 teams will be selected to enter the arena. One real-world problem each. A working product, a startup brand, a chart presentation, a promo reel — and a panel of judges acting as investors.

02 — Selection Round

Selection based on PPT Round. Only top 20 teams get in.

Participation in The Founder's Arena is based on a PPT selection round. Prepare your pitch using the standard official template and upload your finished deck through the registration form. Submissions are evaluated strictly across the four criteria below.

Domains
Domain 1

Rural Development & Social Innovation

Technology and scalable solutions targeting agriculture, rural livelihoods, grassroots education, healthcare access, clean energy, and social empowerment.

Domain 2

FinTech & Financial Inclusion

Innovative financial technology, micro-economy solutions, inclusive banking, accessible digital payments, bookkeeping, and tools for underserved communities.

Evaluation Criteria

Criterion 1

Problem Clarity & Theme Fit

Is the problem clear and real, and does it fit the chosen domain?

Criterion 2

Innovation & Solution Quality

Is the idea original and the solution well thought out?

Criterion 3

Impact & Target Users

Who benefits, and how much difference will it make?

Criterion 4

Technical Feasibility

Can it realistically be built with the proposed tech?

Final scoring weightage will be announced by the organisers.

NOTE

These two domains apply to the selection round only. Domains for the hackathon and Shark Tank pitch will be announced to shortlisted teams at the starting of the competition.

04 — Your Team

Five founders. Five roles.

Every member must understand the whole product — judges may question anyone.

CEO

Chief Executive Officer

  • Introduces the startup, vision, mission and long-term goals
  • Explains the overarching business concept
  • Aligns the founding team and communicates executive strategy

CTO

Chief Technology Officer

  • Explains the tech stack, architecture and feasibility
  • Demos the working product live to investor judges
  • Proves technical execution and scalability

CPO

Chief Product Officer

  • Presents the problem, solution, features and unique value
  • Walks through user personas and experience design
  • Demonstrates how product solves pain points

CFO

Chief Financial Officer

  • Explains the revenue model, unit economics and growth
  • Presents financial assumptions and capital efficiency
  • States the investment ask and how funds will be used

CMO

Chief Marketing Officer

  • Owns branding, visual identity and marketing strategy
  • Defines target audience and customer acquisition
  • Produces and directs the 30–60 second promo reel

05 — The Two Days

Build on Day 1. Pitch on Day 2.

Day 1 — Build Day · November 17

Transform an idea into a functional product and a startup brand.

  1. Build a working product

    Implement core features and test functionality. Product must be complete by 4:30 PM.

  2. Finalise startup brand

    Finalise a unique startup name, logo and cohesive visual identity.

  3. Make physical chart presentation

    Prepare a physical chart paper presentation covering name, domain, problem, solution, features, audience, USP, business model and visuals.

  4. Produce promotional reel

    A 30–60 second promotional reel produced by the CMO that hooks investors and users.

Submit by 4:30 PM: working product · startup name and branding · promotional reel · physical chart. At least 2 teammates must stay at the workstation at all times.

Day 2 — The Investor Pitch · November 18

Convince the investor panel your startup deserves backing.

  1. Introduce your startup

    Startup name, vision, mission, problem and solution.

  2. Live product demo

    Give a live demo of your working product and core features to the judges.

  3. Present physical chart paper

    Walk through your chart paper to visually reinforce your value proposition.

  4. Explain business model & revenue

    Present revenue streams, target market, growth metrics, and customer acquisition.

  5. State investment ask

    State your investment request and exactly how funds will be deployed.

  6. Answer judges' questions as a team

    Defend tech, product, business and financial choices as a united founding team.

Event hours both days: 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM · Seminar Hall, P.A. College of Engineering, Mangaluru
